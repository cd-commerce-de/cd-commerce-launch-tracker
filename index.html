Actual vs. Target

Every week's actual performance, sourced from your Sellerboard and Sellerfox uploads, compared against the Good/Better/Best targets already set for this product.
No products yet
Add a product and upload data from the Upload & Manage tab.

Target shows the Good threshold for that specific week — Sales Price, Units, Avg. CPC, Avg. CR, and Organic Sales % come straight from your weekly forecast upload; every other row is calculated from those 5, the same way the template does it. Each week's Actual cell is colored by which tier that week hit: red below Good, yellow Good to Better, green Better to Best, gold above Best. Rows show "–" for any week with no forecast uploaded yet. Avg. CPC and Number of clicks need a Sellerfox upload for that week to show an Actual value. Organic Sales % (and anything calculated from it) is an approximation — (Units − Ad Orders) ÷ Units — not an exact split.
